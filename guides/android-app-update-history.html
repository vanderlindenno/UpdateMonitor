How to see which apps were updated on your Android phone

Something changed on your phone overnight? Here's how to find out what.

Updated 8 October 2026

Apps on Android update themselves in the background. Usually that's what you want – until something looks different, a feature disappears or your battery suddenly drains faster. Then the question is: which app changed, and when?

Check recent updates in the Play Store

  1. Open the Play Store and tap your profile picture at the top right.
  2. Choose Manage apps & device and open the Manage tab.
  3. Tap the sort option and choose Recently updated.

To see the version an app has now, go to Settings → Apps, tap the app and scroll down to the version number.

The limits of that list

  • It only covers apps from the Play Store – not apps from other stores, APK files or your employer.
  • It doesn't show the version an app had before the update, or the exact time.
  • It doesn't show apps you removed, or what changed in an app's permissions.
  • Android itself keeps no update log at all.

Frequently asked questions

Can I see updates from before I installed UpdateMonitor?

No. Android doesn't keep that information, so UpdateMonitor can only record changes from the moment you first open it.

How do I stop apps from updating automatically?

In the Play Store, tap your profile picture → Settings → Network preferences → Auto-update apps and choose Don't auto-update apps. For a single app, open its Play Store page, tap the three dots and turn off Enable auto update.
